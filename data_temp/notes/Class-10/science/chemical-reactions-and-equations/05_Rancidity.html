Rancidity

Rancidity is the process where fats and oils become unpleasant due to oxidation.

Causes

  • Oxidation by oxygen
  • Moisture
  • Microorganisms
  • Light

Effects

  • Unpleasant smell and taste
  • Food becomes unfit

Prevention

  • Store in airtight containers
  • Add antioxidants like BHA, BHT
  • Refrigeration
  • Flush with nitrogen

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Rancidity is auto-oxidation of unsaturated fats. Antioxidants donate electrons to prevent chain reactions.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't confuse with spoilage by bacteria. Avoid saying all foods rancid; mainly fats. Remember antioxidants like vitamin E.
