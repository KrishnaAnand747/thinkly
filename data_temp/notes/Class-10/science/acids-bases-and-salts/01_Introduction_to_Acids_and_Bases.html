Introduction to Acids and Bases

Acids and bases are two important classes of compounds that have distinct properties and play crucial roles in various chemical reactions and biological processes.

Acids

  • Taste sour
  • Turn blue litmus red
  • pH less than 7
  • Release H+ ions in aqueous solution

Bases

  • Taste bitter
  • Turn red litmus blue
  • pH greater than 7
  • Release OH- ions in aqueous solution

Neutral Substances: Have pH 7, like water.

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Acids and bases are not just about taste; they are defined by their ability to donate or accept protons. The Arrhenius theory focuses on ions, while Brønsted-Lowry considers proton transfer, providing deeper understanding.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember "Sour Acid, Bitter Base" for taste. Use litmus colors: Acids red, bases blue. For pH, acids <7, bases >7. Practice identifying from examples like HCl (acid), NaOH (base).

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't confuse sour taste with all acids; some are corrosive. Avoid thinking all bases are alkalis; ammonia is a base but not alkali. Remember that pH scale is logarithmic.
