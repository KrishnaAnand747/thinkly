Functional Groups

Functional groups are specific groups of atoms that determine the chemical properties of organic compounds.

Common Functional Groups

  • Alcohol (-OH): e.g., CH3OH
  • Aldehyde (-CHO): e.g., CH3CHO
  • Ketone (-CO-): e.g., CH3COCH3
  • Carboxylic Acid (-COOH): e.g., CH3COOH
  • Halogen (-X): e.g., CH3Cl

Homologous Series

Series of compounds with same functional group, general formula, similar properties, differ by CH2.

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Functional groups dictate reactivity; compounds with same group have similar reactions. Homologous series show gradation in properties like boiling point.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember suffixes: -ol for alcohol, -al for aldehyde, -oic acid for carboxylic. Homologous: same formula pattern, differ by CH2.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't confuse aldehyde and ketone; aldehyde has H. Avoid wrong naming; ethanol is CH3CH2OH. Remember homologous increase in mass by 14.
