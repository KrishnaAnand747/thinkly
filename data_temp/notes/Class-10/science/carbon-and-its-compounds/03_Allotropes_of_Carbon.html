Allotropes of Carbon

Allotropes are different forms of the same element with different physical properties due to different arrangements of atoms.

Diamond

  • Hardest substance
  • Each C bonded to 4 others in tetrahedral structure
  • Used in cutting tools

Graphite

  • Soft, slippery
  • Layered structure, each C bonded to 3 others
  • Conducts electricity
  • Used in pencils, lubricants

Fullerenes

  • Spherical molecules like C60
  • Used in nanotechnology

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

The difference in properties comes from bonding: Diamond has strong 3D bonds, graphite has weak interlayer forces. Fullerenes show carbon can form closed cages.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember "Diamond Hard, Graphite Soft". Diamond: 3D, sp3. Graphite: layers, sp2. Fullerene: C60, soccer ball.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't say diamond conducts; it doesn't. Avoid confusing buckminsterfullerene with other fullerenes. Remember allotropes have same element, different structure.
