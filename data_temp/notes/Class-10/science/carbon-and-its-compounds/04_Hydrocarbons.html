Hydrocarbons

Hydrocarbons are compounds containing only carbon and hydrogen. They are classified as saturated and unsaturated.

Saturated Hydrocarbons (Alkanes)

  • General formula: CnH2n+2
  • Single bonds
  • Example: CH4 (methane), C2H6 (ethane)

Unsaturated Hydrocarbons

  • Alkenes: CnH2n, double bond
  • Alkynes: CnH2n-2, triple bond
  • Examples: C2H4 (ethene), C2H2 (ethyne)

Combustion

Produce CO2 and H2O, release energy.

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Unsaturated hydrocarbons undergo addition reactions due to pi bonds, while saturated undergo substitution. This leads to different chemical behaviors.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember formulas: Alkanes +2, Alkenes same, Alkynes -2. Saturated: single bonds, unsaturated: double/triple. Combustion: CO2 + H2O.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't confuse alkene (ene) with alkyne (yne). Avoid wrong formulas; count carbons. Remember ethyne is acetylene.
