Anomalies in Periodic Table

Some elements don't fit perfectly due to unique properties.

Position of Hydrogen

  • Can be placed in group 1 or 17
  • Similar to alkali metals (H+) and halogens (H-)
  • Unique: Forms diatomic gas

Reasons for Periodicity

  • Similar outer electron configuration
  • Repetition every 2, 8, 18, 32 electrons

Other Anomalies

  • Transition metals in d-block
  • Lanthanoids and actinoids

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Hydrogen's anomaly reflects its unique position as the first element. Periodicity arises from quantum shells filling predictably.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember H in both 1 and 17. Periodicity: Similar valence electrons. Anomalies: H, transition metals.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't place H only in group 1. Avoid confusing anomalies with trends. Remember periodicity is due to electron configuration.
