Chemical Properties of Metals

Metals react with oxygen, water and acids to form new substances.

Reaction with Oxygen

Metals react with oxygen to form metal oxides.

Figure: Magnesium burns in air to form magnesium oxide

Reaction with Acids

Metals react with dilute acids to form salt and hydrogen gas.

💡 Curious Mind Insight

Gold and platinum do not react with acids easily, which is why they do not corrode!
