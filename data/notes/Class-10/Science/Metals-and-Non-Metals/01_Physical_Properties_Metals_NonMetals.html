Physical Properties of Metals and Non-Metals

Metals and non-metals show very different physical properties which help us identify them easily.

Metals

  • Shiny (lustrous)
  • Good conductors of heat and electricity
  • Malleable and ductile

Non-Metals

  • Dull in appearance
  • Poor conductors of heat and electricity
  • Brittle in nature

💡 Curious Mind Insight

Mercury is a metal but exists in liquid form, while graphite is a non-metal that conducts electricity!
