Acids, Bases and Indicators

Acids are sour and turn blue litmus red. Bases are bitter and turn red litmus blue.

Figure: Litmus paper showing acidic and basic behaviour

Types of Indicators

  • Natural: Litmus, Turmeric
  • Synthetic: Phenolphthalein, Methyl Orange
  • Olfactory: Onion, Clove, Vanilla

🧠 Curious Minds

Turmeric turns red-brown in bases.

Soap is basic — that’s why turmeric stains change colour.

Indicators avoid tasting dangerous chemicals.
