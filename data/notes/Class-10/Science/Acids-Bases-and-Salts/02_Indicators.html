Indicators

Indicators are substances that change color in acidic or basic solutions. They help in identifying the nature of a solution.

Natural Indicators

  • Litmus: Red in acid, blue in base
  • Turmeric: Yellow in neutral, red in base
  • China Rose: Dark pink in base, green in acid

Synthetic Indicators

  • Methyl Orange: Red in acid, yellow in base
  • Phenolphthalein: Colorless in acid, pink in base

Olfactory Indicators

Change smell: Onion (smells in base), vanilla (smells in acid).

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Indicators work due to structural changes in molecules. For example, litmus contains anthocyanin which changes structure with pH, altering light absorption and thus color.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Mnemonic for litmus: "Litmus Red Acid, Blue Base". For phenolphthalein: "Pink in Base, Clear in Acid". Remember olfactory: Onion smells in base, vanilla in acid.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't use litmus for strong bases; it may not change properly. Avoid confusing methyl orange (red acid) with phenolphthalein (colorless acid). Remember that indicators have a pH range for color change.
