Effects of Oxidation Reactions in Everyday Life

Oxidation reactions have various effects in daily life, some beneficial, some harmful.

Rusting of Iron

4Fe + 3O2 + 6H2O → 2Fe2O3·3H2O
Slow oxidation causing damage.

Browning of Fruits

Enzymes oxidize phenols to brown pigments.
Prevented by vitamin C or acids.

Combustion

Rapid oxidation releasing heat and light.
Example: Burning of fuels.

Respiration

Oxidation of glucose in cells for energy.

Figure: Oxidation in everyday life

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Oxidation is loss of electrons, but in terms of oxygen, it's addition. In food, it leads to spoilage, but controlled oxidation preserves some foods.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember rust formula. Browning: Enzyme + O2. Combustion: Fuel + O2 → CO2 + H2O + heat. Respiration: C6H12O6 + O2 → CO2 + H2O + energy.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't confuse rusting with corrosion; rust is specific. Avoid saying all oxidation is bad; respiration is essential. Remember prevention methods.
