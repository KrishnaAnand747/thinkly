Chemical Equations

Chemical equations represent chemical reactions using symbols and formulas.

Writing Chemical Equations

  • Reactants on left, products on right
  • Separated by arrow (→)
  • Use symbols: (s) solid, (l) liquid, (g) gas, (aq) aqueous

Balancing Equations

  • Same number of atoms on both sides
  • Change coefficients, not subscripts
  • Example: H2 + O2 → 2H2O

Types

  • Skeleton: Unbalanced
  • Balanced: Equal atoms

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Equations must obey law of conservation of mass; atoms can't be created or destroyed. Balancing is like solving algebraic equations.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember symbols: (s), (l), (g), (aq). Balance by hit and trial: Start with metals, then non-metals. Check atoms count.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't change formulas; only coefficients. Avoid forgetting states. Remember arrow direction: reactants to products.
