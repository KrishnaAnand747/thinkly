What is Corrosion?

Corrosion is the slow eating away of metals due to chemical reactions with environment.

Causes

  • Moisture and oxygen
  • Acids in atmosphere
  • Salt in air

Examples

  • Rusting of iron
  • Tarnishing of silver
  • Green coating on copper

Prevention

  • Painting
  • Galvanizing
  • Alloying

Figure: Corrosion and prevention

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Corrosion is electrochemical: Anode loses electrons, cathode gains. Noble metals don't corrode due to high reduction potential.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember causes: O2 + H2O. Prevention: Paint, Zinc, Alloy. Rust: Fe2O3.xH2O.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't confuse with rust; corrosion is general. Avoid saying all metals corrode; gold doesn't. Remember electrochemical nature.
