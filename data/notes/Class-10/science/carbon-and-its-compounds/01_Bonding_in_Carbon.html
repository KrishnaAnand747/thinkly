Bonding in Carbon

Carbon has unique bonding properties that allow it to form a vast number of compounds. It is tetravalent and forms covalent bonds.

Electronic Configuration

Carbon has atomic number 6: 1s² 2s² 2p². It needs 4 electrons to achieve octet.

Covalent Bonding

Carbon shares electrons with other atoms to form covalent bonds. It can form single, double, or triple bonds.

Tetravalency

Carbon has 4 valence electrons, so it forms 4 bonds.

Catenation

Ability to form long chains by bonding with itself.

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Carbon's ability to form multiple bonds and chains leads to isomers, where compounds with same formula have different structures. This is key to organic chemistry diversity.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't confuse valence with valency; carbon has valency 4. Avoid thinking carbon only forms single bonds; it can form multiple. Remember octet rule.
