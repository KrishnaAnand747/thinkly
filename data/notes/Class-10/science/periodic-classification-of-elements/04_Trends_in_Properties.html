Trends in Properties

Properties show periodic trends across periods and down groups.

Valency

  • Across period: Increases to 4 then decreases to 0
  • Down group: Remains same

Atomic Size

  • Across period: Decreases
  • Down group: Increases

Metallic Character

  • Across period: Decreases
  • Down group: Increases

Non-Metallic Character

  • Across period: Increases
  • Down group: Decreases

Ionization Energy

  • Across period: Increases
  • Down group: Decreases

Figure: Periodic trends

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Trends are due to electron configuration. Atomic size decreases across period due to increased nuclear charge without shell addition.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember "Period Left to Right: Size ↓, Metallic ↓, Non-metallic ↑". "Group Top to Bottom: Size ↑, Metallic ↑". Valency: Group number for main groups.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't confuse atomic size with atomic mass. Avoid saying metallic increases across period; it decreases. Remember exceptions like Be in IE.
