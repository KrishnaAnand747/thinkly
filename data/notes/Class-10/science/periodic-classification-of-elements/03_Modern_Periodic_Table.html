Modern Periodic Table

The modern periodic table is based on atomic number and has 18 groups and 7 periods.

Modern Periodic Law

Properties of elements are periodic function of their atomic numbers.

Structure

  • 18 groups (1-18)
  • 7 periods (1-7)
  • 4 blocks: s, p, d, f

Groups

  • 1: Alkali metals
  • 2: Alkaline earth metals
  • 17: Halogens
  • 18: Noble gases

Periods

Increase in atomic number left to right.

Figure: Modern periodic table

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Atomic number reflects protons, determining electron configuration. This leads to periodicity in properties every 2, 8, 18, 32 electrons.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Remember 18 groups, 7 periods. Groups: 1 Alkali, 2 Earth, 17 Halo, 18 Noble. Periods increase atomic number.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't confuse groups with periods. Avoid old 8 groups; it's 18. Remember atomic number basis.
