Reactivity Series

The reactivity series is a list of metals arranged in decreasing order of their reactivity.

Figure: Reactivity series of common metals

Extraction of Metals

  • Highly reactive metals – electrolysis
  • Moderately reactive metals – reduction
  • Low reactive metals – found in free state
💡 Curious Mind Insight

Aluminium is highly reactive, yet it does not corrode easily due to a protective oxide layer!
