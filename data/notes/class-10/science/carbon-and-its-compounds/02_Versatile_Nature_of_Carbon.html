Versatile Nature of Carbon

Carbon's versatility arises from its ability to form bonds with itself and other elements, leading to millions of compounds.

Reasons for Versatility

  • Tetravalency
  • Catenation
  • Formation of multiple bonds
  • Isomerism

Types of Compounds

  • Organic compounds: Contain C-H bonds
  • Inorganic: CO, CO2, carbonates

Importance

Forms basis of life, fuels, materials like plastics.

Figure: Versatility of carbon

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

Carbon's small size allows strong bonds, enabling complex structures. This versatility is why carbon is central to biochemistry and industry.
