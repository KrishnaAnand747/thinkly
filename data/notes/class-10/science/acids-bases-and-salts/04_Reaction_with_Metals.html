Reaction with Metals

Acids react with metals to produce salts and hydrogen gas. The reactivity depends on the metal's position in the reactivity series.

General Reaction

Metal + Acid → Salt + Hydrogen
e.g., Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl2 + H2

Conditions

  • Metal must be above hydrogen in reactivity series
  • Dilute acids are used
  • Reaction is faster with more reactive metals

Examples

  • Mg + 2HCl → MgCl2 + H2
  • Fe + H2SO4 → FeSO4 + H2

Note: Noble metals like Au, Ag do not react.

💡 Insights for Inquisitive Minds

The reaction involves oxidation of metal and reduction of H+ to H2. Metals below hydrogen don't react because they are less reactive. Nitric acid may not produce H2 due to oxidation.

🎯 Scoring & Study Tips

Use reactivity series to predict reactions. Remember the equation format: Metal + 2HCl → MetalCl2 + H2. Test for H2: burns with pop sound.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Don't assume all metals react; check reactivity series. Avoid writing wrong formulas; balance properly. Remember that concentrated acids may react differently.
